How It Works

Teach yourself by answering questions,
not by reading answers.

Velocity uses Socratic dialogue combined with a probabilistic model that tracks your exact level of mastery per concept.

01

Pick a DSA concept

Choose from 18 nodes across the full DSA curriculum. The skill map shows your mastery per node and recommends where to start based on your prerequisites.

02

Your AI tutor asks, not tells

Vector or Aura probes your mental model with open questions. When you're wrong, they don't correct you — they ask the question that exposes the flaw in your own reasoning.

03

Mastery updates in real time

Every response updates your mastery score using Bayesian Knowledge Tracing. Wrong answers decrease it. Strong reasoning increases it. Guesses don't fool it.

04

Gaps surface automatically

When you struggle, Velocity identifies the specific misconception and routes you to the prerequisite concept that's actually missing — before you waste time on the wrong thing.

Visual skill map with mastery arcs per node. Weakness bars by category. Every misconception from past sessions stored and surfaced.

Auto-Escalation
